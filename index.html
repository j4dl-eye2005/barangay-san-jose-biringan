Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026  ·  Academic project — no real data collected

This portal lets residents create accounts, submit proof of residency, and request barangay documents. We are committed to handling your personal information responsibly and in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Information we collect

We may collect the following when you use the portal:

  • Account details (name, email, phone number, home address)
  • Proof-of-residency files you upload (PDF, DOCX, JPG, or PNG)
  • Document request details — type, purpose, and status
  • Date and time of registrations and requests
  • IP address, browser, and device info for security purposes
  • Server logs and error records
  • Anything you share when contacting us

How we use it

We use this information to operate the portal, verify resident accounts, process document requests, send email notifications, maintain security, and comply with legal requirements.

Accounts & uploads

Registration details and uploaded proof-of-residency files are reviewed by barangay administrators. Please only upload documents that are yours and avoid including information not needed for verification.

Data retention

We keep account records, files, request history, and logs for as long as needed to operate the portal and meet legal obligations. Fraudulent or inappropriate content may be removed at any time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No internet service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

Third-party services

We may use hosting or email-delivery services to run the portal. These providers may process personal information under their own policies.

Cookies

We may use cookies to keep you signed in and improve performance. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may stop working.

Your rights

Under RA 10173, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To remove your account or an uploaded document, contact us below.

Changes

We may update this policy at any time. Changes will appear on this page with a new date.
